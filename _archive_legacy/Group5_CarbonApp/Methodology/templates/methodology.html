Methodology

Our carbon calculator estimates transportation emissions using simple, transparent formulas and fixed emission factors.

The objective is to make climate impact easier to understand through everyday transport choices.

Our numbers and formulas

Public transport:

\[ CO2 = Distance \times Emission\ Factor \]

Private transport:

\[ CO2 = Distance \times Emission\ Factor_{passenger} \]

Human Power

Walking and cycling are considered zero direct transport emissions in this methodology.

\[ EF_{walk/bike} = 0\ g/km \]

Used for walk and bike options in the app.

Units used

$D$
Distance traveled (km)
$EF$
Emission factor (g/km or g/passenger-km)
$CO2$
Estimated emissions (gCO2e)
